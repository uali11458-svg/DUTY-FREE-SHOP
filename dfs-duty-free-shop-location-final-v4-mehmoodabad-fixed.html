📜 Terms & Conditions

DFS - Duty Free Shop istemal karne se pehle ye points parh lein

  • DFS ek open marketplace hai — koi bhi login kar ke apna product list kar sakta hai aur khareed sakta hai.
  • Har listing us seller ki apni zimmedari hai — DFS sirf platform faraham karta hai, khud koi product nahi baichta.
  • Payment aur delivery seedha buyer aur seller ke darmiyan (Call/WhatsApp par) tay hoti hai — DFS iska hissa nahi hai.
  • Ghalat, jaali, ya ghair-qanooni product list karna mana hai — aisi ads 🚩 Report ki ja sakti hain aur hata di jayengi.
  • Har user apni account details (password, login) khud mehfooz rakhne ka zimmedar hai.
  • DFS kisi bhi waqt koi listing, account, ya feature tabdeel ya band karne ka haq rakhta hai.
  • Kisi bhi deal se pehle product, seller, aur payment tareeqe ko khud achi tarah check kar lein.

🔒 Privacy Policy

Aapki maloomat kaise mehfooz aur istemal hoti hai

  • Signup ke waqt sirf naam, email, aur password liya jata hai — koi extra personal detail nahi mangi jati.
  • Aapka contact number sirf tab dikhta hai jab aap khud apni listing mein daalein (buyers ke sath share karne ke liye).
  • Chat messages sirf aap aur doosre user ke darmiyan hote hain — DFS unhein kisi teesre ko nahi deta.
  • Profile picture aur naam sirf aapki apni listings ke sath dikhaye jate hain, taake buyers seller ko pehchan sakein.
  • Aapka data kisi bhi advertiser ya teesri party ko becha ya share nahi kiya jata.
  • Aap apni profile, products, ya account kabhi bhi khud delete/update kar sakte hain.
